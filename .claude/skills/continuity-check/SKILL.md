# Continuity Check

## Inputs
The draft; `source/INDEX.md` (precedence); the consolidated bible; the relevant beat sheet; `editorial/continuity-ledger.md`; `editorial/canon-decisions.md`; every `approved.md` that precedes this chapter.

## Check
1. **Names and terms.** Anna, not Anya (unless it's a deliberate endearment per the bible's naming note). "The Resonator," never "the Resonant." White Vein used only for the mineral seam. Deka. Lampshell AUVs. Shulganite (not named before it's discovered or named in-story).
2. **Characters.** Ages, nationalities, backgrounds, relationships, and speech patterns against the bible and the ledger. Who knows what, as of this chapter.
3. **Timeline.** Season, dates, elapsed time, daylight, weather continuity within the chapter.
4. **World rules.** Resonator narrative rules (never explains itself, never communicates, never anthropomorphic). Biological effects stay subtle and statistical. Physical behavior stays consistent with earlier chapters.
5. **Geography.** Places named, distances, travel times. Ufa, Shulgan basin, and so on.
6. **Earlier approved text.** Nothing contradicts it.
7. **New inventions.** Every named person, place, object, institution, or backstory fact that isn't in the sources.

## Output
A table: `item | draft says | canon/ledger says | source | severity (contradiction / unverified / new invention)`. Put it inside the editorial review. New inventions go into the ledger **only after approval**, as `proposed` entries until then.
