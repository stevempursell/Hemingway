## Remaining weaknesses, ranked

1. **The channel is described twice.** First from the shelf in the author's paragraph, then again at the bottom ("A cut in the pale limestone… overhang caged in rusted mesh… survey tunnel"). *Fix:* cut the second description down to the overhang and tunnel only, or to the laugh and "exactly where the map had promised."
2. **The "Safe" gag raises a question the story may need to answer.** Khasanov's biography is one of warnings. Why did he write *Safe* beside this channel? It's funny, and the irony works. If the author has a reason (a dry-season survey, a dry joke, someone else's hand), a later chapter with Zamir could pay it off. **Author's call.** No change recommended in the prologue.
3. **Khasanov's "braver than you are sensible… It wears off"** is close to an aphorism. It's in character (dry, warm, generational) and in dialogue, where the voice profile allows it. Watch that Zamir doesn't collect more lines like it.
4. **"It should have been pretty. He didn't like looking at it."** This is the one deliberate short button left. It carries the beat sheet's "Not beautiful. Unsettling." Keep it as the single allowed one.
5. **s03 is still the longest scene relative to its job** (1,805 words vs a 1,700 budget). The three tests are now plain and quick. If anything is trimmed, trim the Deka report and vending machine beat, not Alina's texts.
