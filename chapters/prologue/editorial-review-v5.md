## Findings, ranked

**1. Orphan from the v3 rebuild: "That's a Khasanov thing you're doing with your hand."** (s01)
The lecture memory that set up Khasanov's raised-palm gesture was cut in v3. Alina's joke now refers to a gesture the reader has never seen. It half-works (the reader can infer Khasanov does odd things), but it lands as a reference to something offstage.
*Fix:* one clause in Timur's head right after her line. For example, Khasanov at the front of a lecture hall with his palm up to a draft nobody else could feel. Or cut "with your hand" and keep only "Is that a Khasanov thing?"

**2. The new job beat (B1) doesn't pay off.** (s01 → s03)
Timur comes for the orange stain, reaches for the probe "to start on the stain," gets diverted, and never samples it. The diversion is right. But the story drops the job entirely, so B1's professional motivation fades after one paragraph.
*Fix (one line, s03):* while waiting on the metals, he realizes he never sampled the stain he drove five hours for. That's human and wry, and it keeps him a contamination chemist first. It also sharpens the science quietly: clear water running right beside contaminated drainage and carrying none of it. **No new background paragraph.**

**3. The same comparison twice: "the purified water the lab…"** (s02 l.127 and s03 l.179)
In s02 it's a joke (the coffee tap). In s03 it's the result. The second use repeats the image, and its "three filters and an ultraviolet lamp" description is the more technical of the two.
*Fix:* keep the s02 joke. In s03 just call back to it ("It read like the coffee tap.") and drop the filters-and-lamp clause. That's lighter for a lay reader too.

**4. Simile cluster in s03's first ~500 words.** Five figures in quick succession: attic furniture, the purified tap, tea with no tea, a mended coat, corrected results. The draft overall is moderate (about 13 real similes, about 2.5 per 1,000 words). This one stretch reads busy.
*Fix:* lose one or two. The attic-furniture fans and "tea with no tea" are the keepers. The mended coat can go, since the corrected-results image does the same work better.

**5. Consistency nits**
- "the fallen fence" (s03 l.225) vs "the broken fence" (s01 ×2). Use "broken."
- s01 l.19: "photocopied so many times that the contour lines had gone furry, gone over first in his pencil": "gone furry, gone over" stutters. *Fix:* "…the contour lines had gone furry. He had gone over it first in pencil: arrows…"

**6. Watch items (no change recommended)**
- *Soviet survey tunnel beside the anomaly* (B1). Proximity is canon (the beat sheet: channels "connected to older Soviet survey tunnels"), and nothing implies the tunnels touched the system. Passes the T4 check. Keep it that way in later chapters.
- *Khasanov: "braver than you are sensible… It wears off."* Still his only aphoristic line. Fine.
- *"the whole mountain had drawn in a breath."* The single irrational flicker. Fine.
- *The field spectrometer's first appearance is at its uncanny moment* (s03). Planting it earlier would be "Chekhov's inventory." The line "left it upside down on a paper towel to dry" implies it was in the field. Keep.
- *Deka appears only through objects* (the crate logo, the quarterly report). The scholarship isn't stated, but the reader infers Timur answers to Deka. Acceptable, and restrained.
