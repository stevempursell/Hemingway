## Beat changes (three, all small)

**B1. Timur arrives on a concrete environmental job** (brief §6; impact report C6).
*Problem:* since v2, s01 never says why he's at this channel beyond Khasanov's map and curiosity. His contamination work only appears in s03.
*Change:* in s01, one or two anchored lines (in dialogue or a thought prompted by what's in front of him) make this channel a **station in his contamination study**: the old survey tunnel drains here, and his thesis follows what abandoned Soviet workings leak underground. He's here to sample that. The clear water is what **diverts** him from the job. **Heavy metals only, no radioactivity** (CM9). No new background paragraph (the "background stays in the room" rule).

**B2. "Corrected" is plainly Timur's metaphor** (rule 4; impact report C7).
*Current s03 line:* "It looked as though the corrections had been made in the water itself." That's already framed as his impression.
*Change:* keep it, and check the sentences around it so none states a finding beyond his own data. **Beat sheet Part III's "impossible corrective properties" is retired as a beat description.** The beat now reads: "results that contradict everything in his own data, which he can only describe to himself as *corrected*."

**B3. The karst is a place, not a clue machine** (brief §6).
*Assessment:* the flood, the crawl, the cold, and the trap already give it a physical reality of its own. *Optional:* one ordinary, non-anomalous cave detail in the chamber (old flood debris on a ledge, a survey mark, mud ridged by past floods) so not every observation is a clue. **Writer's call when making the edit.**
