## The four threads. Keep them distinct, always

| Thread | Status | Statement |
|---|---|---|
| T1. Human damage | CONFIRMED | Soviet-era (and later) human activity causes **real environmental damage** in the region |
| T2. Support | CONFIRMED | **The Resonator** is a system that artificially supports Earth's ecosystem and habitability. The karst is connected to its operation and is where humans can begin to observe its effects |
| T3. Failure | CONFIRMED | The Resonator is **under strain** and is **eventually revealed to be failing**. Revealed gradually; a late-novel truth |
| T4. Not the Soviets | CONFIRMED | **Soviet activity did not damage the Resonator or cause its failure.** The failure has a **larger, longer-running cause**. Not yet developed. **Do not invent it, imply it, or reveal it** |

The novel may connect these threads in other ways later. It must **never** collapse them into "humans damaged an ancient system, and now it's retaliating."

## Confirmed rules

1. **Name:** "the Resonator." "The Resonant" stays retired (author, 2026-10-15). The White Vein is a local physical manifestation, never an alias.
2. **Deka's Shulganite extraction is an accelerant** (author, 2026-10-15). The failure is already under way from its larger cause. Extraction adds strain and speeds the collapse (this keeps the Books 2–4 conflict). Two causes, never the same cause. The bible's "Curse" should be read as *extraction accelerates the collapse*, not *extraction causes the failure*.
3. **The water is a window, not the whole mystery.** Its anomalies are observations of a larger process, not a set of separate powers.
4. **Purity is a signature or side effect**, not the mechanism of ecosystem support.
5. **Biological effects: the water does it** (author, 2026-10-15). The water carries real healing effects, and through it people are **metaphysically connected to the Resonator's healing nature**. **This never needs to be stated explicitly**, and it should not be stated: no character explains it and the narration doesn't assert it. It lives in effects, impressions, and what people can't account for.
6. **Early phenomena support multiple plausible interpretations:** unusual chemistry, a geological anomaly, an environmental remediation process, a hidden system. None should make the complete explanation obvious.
7. **The Vodyanoy** stays canon as the Resonator's **automated protective response to intrusion**. Frame it as a reflex, never as vengeance and never as a response to Soviet harm.
8. **Accounting rule:** any explanation must account for its consequences. If something is removed from water, the story eventually has to consider where it goes (OPEN, below). "Purification" is never a universal fix.

## Confirmed by the author (was writer's reading)

- **W1 (CONFIRMED 2026-10-15, "Yes, bounded"). The healing is real but bounded.** The brief also forbids an omnipotent system that "can heal any damage without limits." The existing narrative rule (retired-Resonant doc: biological effects "subtle and statistical… no instant healing") is therefore kept, and the healing is **limited and weakening as the strain grows (T3)**. That's also how the healing can foreshadow the failure.

## Forbidden implications (validation checklist, run on every plan, draft, and canon edit)

1. The Soviets damaged, woke, or triggered the Resonator.
2. Water purity alone explains ecosystem support.
3. Any anomaly has already been explained to the reader.
4. The Resonator is omnipotent or heals without limits.
5. The late answer (the failure, its cause, the metaphysical connection) is revealed early or stated outright.
6. The Vodyanoy as retaliation or vengeance.
