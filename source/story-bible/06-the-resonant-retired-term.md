## Core Definition (background)
An ancient, non-terrestrial planetary stabilization system in the flooded karst of the southern Urals. Initially read as an anomalous geological structure; neither machine nor organism in any conventional sense. Humanity first identifies it through measurable side effects: impossible water purity; anomalous conductivity behavior; statistically improbable ecological stability; low-frequency harmonic emissions; and the existence of Shulganite. Its true purpose remains unknown throughout the series. Leading theory: a planetary equilibrium mechanism that gently biases systems toward survivability over immense timescales. **It does not "break" natural laws.** Humanity mistakes its effects for normal planetary behavior because its influence predates recorded civilization.

## Observed Effects
**Hydrological:** impossible purity gradients; near-total absence of expected contaminants; suppressed isotopic irregularity; abnormal conductive behavior. *Certain underground tributaries resist turbulent mixing with surrounding runoff for brief periods.* Water systems near its influence recover from contamination significantly faster than models allow. Surrounding karst displays fluid behavior inconsistent with conventional pressure and flow dynamics.

**Biological:** slightly reduced cellular degradation; unusually resilient DNA repair; suppressed inflammatory disease rates; slower telomere deterioration variance. **These effects are subtle and statistically distributed, not miraculous: no immortality, no instant healing, no overt "fountain of youth."**

**Geological/Planetary:** may contribute to long-term climate stabilization, suppression of runaway ecological collapse, damping of magnetic instability, mitigation of catastrophic feedback. Earth appears anomalously stable relative to expected planetary models.

## Harmonic Phenomena
Persistent low-frequency harmonic activity, initially read as seismic resonance, later understood to be structured and adaptive: interference with instrumentation; pressure-wave propagation through groundwater; localized disruption of electronics; synchronized vibration events; "Sonic Pulse" defensive responses following extraction. No known model fully explains the harmonics; no consensus.

## Relationship to Shulganite
Byproduct or structural derivative. Room-temperature superconductivity; near-lossless energy transfer; extraordinary lattice stability; scalable industrial potential. Extraction provokes increasingly severe responses.

## The Alignment Hypothesis (highly speculative / partially suppressed)
Not locally powered but linked to something larger (planetary alignment, phase overlap, field interaction); maintaining synchronization rather than generating equilibrium; possibly degrading over astronomical timescales. Unproven and politically explosive. [Not in the consolidated bible. Status: background idea, **not canon** until the author confirms.]

## Human Misinterpretation (stages)
1. Geological Curiosity → 2. Technological Miracle → 3. Environmental System → 4. Existential Revelation.

## Narrative Rules (IMPORTANT)
The Resonator should NEVER: fully explain itself; communicate directly; become anthropomorphic; or resolve into simple "alien technology." Its power comes from: partial observability; contradictory interpretations; measurable but incomprehensible behavior. Characters may understand effects, correlations, consequences. They never fully understand purpose, origin, or operating mechanism.
