# Borderline (Rio Rico) — Series Treatment

> Verbatim from Drive (Jun 2012).

Rio Rico - "Borderline"

In Rio Rico, Arizona, Neil has had to move back after not being able to finish ASU/UofA/NAU. His presiding belief is that it just doesn't matter. He doesn't feel he needs a degree to do something with his life. He ran out of money and drive, flunked college and now is responsible for working with his dad's landscaping company while his mom works at a local resort as a masseuse. He hopes to pay off his student loans in peace until the world eventually just learns to leave him alone.

His neighbor, the recently layed off Carlin, and him quickly become neighborly. They have a shared resentment of society. When is father Bruce comes down with valley fever, Neil enlists Carlin to help him with the landscaping.

Bruce: "It's two houses. Two. That's all. You can't possibly mess this up. PLEASE don't mess this up."

Inadvertently, Carlin and Neil forget to set the brake on the tractor and sort of maybe run over someone that was hiding in the irrigation canal. Appearing homeless, they quickly decide they have to hide the body. Carlin is too pretty for jail, and Neil would probably like it but should still not go there anyways. So with the help of Bruce's truck and landscaping equipment, they drive to the middle of the desert and leave the body, while narrowly avoiding a local Border patrol. But they were watched from afar.

Two days later, while seemingly having forgotten about it, Neil is called over by Carlin to deal with his neuroses about having killed a person. Carlin is scared of being found out, he's panicking as a normal person would. They go for a beer and, once again borrowing Bruce's truck, they come back to find a duffel bag of unmarked cash in the back, $5,000. And a note saying the contract was completed.

With some help from Amanda, Neil's old friend who "used to be a bit more frumpy, is less frumpy now" helps his "thesis research" into Cartel's by finding out that there's a series of wanted cartel targets in the southwest that have eluded capture long enough to make it into the states. Her Brother, Sam, interns for the Border patrol and lets them know the body of a prominent cartel enemy was found. Convenient timing, sure. The gears turn.

Neil: "WE killed him. Carlin. We deserve the money. We could... we could do it again."

No remorse kicks in, and the two, under the guise of Rio Rico Landscaping LTD employees, begin to keep an eye out for suspicious criminal activity, while enlisting Amanda to find them a list of wanted targets. For, ahem... research.

It's two parts Reaper, one part Dexter, and a solid mix of Justified all rolled into one. it's the Anti-Glee, it's the literal death knoll for all the saccharine sweet WB teen drama's. Dealing with themes based on death, remorse, ambition, debt, sacrifice and murder come second place to exploring the wasteland that is the border towns like Rio Rico. When far enough outside of civilization, you can find yourself outside of the law. And maybe a little out of your mind.

Neil Fleece – Lost his scholarship due to dropping grades, attendance and a particularly bad interaction with a professor's wife, Neil moves back to Rio Rico having all but given up on the societal contract and hoping to pay off his student debt in peace.

Carlin Martinez - Recently laid off, he befriends Neil and soon begins to search for work, eventually hoping to convince Bruce to hire him as an extra set of hands. His family looks to him for support. His wife works with Toni, Neil's mother, at the resort.

Bruce Fleece – Neil's dad, owns and operates RRLL, and is having trouble accepting his songs resignation to work with his hands, thinking him more of a "soft plushy thinker type"

Toni Fleece – Neil's mother, obsessed with the drama she overhears from the local resort massage tables.

Amanda Craynor, Less frumpy and a good friend of Neil's from high school. She never left Rio Rico, he came back.

Sam Craynor, Border Patrol Intern and Amanda's brother

Lt. Hansen Mack, Border Sergeant and Sam's boss.
