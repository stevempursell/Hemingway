TEASER

FADE IN:

INT. HOTEL LOBBY - DAY

Attendants stand at the sleek and posh front desk with lines of incoming guests. The guests look like those people waiting in line at the food stamp office.

BARRY HOWARD (35), a blue oxford, Dockers, and New Balance kind of guy stands ready for the next guest. Ladies would give Barry a solid seven out of ten. He blows his nose into a rag and wipes his brow with it. Make that a solid six.

A very OLD GUEST wearing a $5000 suit talks with a front desk attendant, visibly upset. Barry notices and steps in. The attendant steps away, shell-shocked.

BARRY: Hello sir, thank you so much for staying with us at The Palace. My name is Barry Howard, I am the manager here. How may I assist you today?

OLD GUEST: Well the problem is I needed a weekend away from the wife and I am just looking for some "entertainment".

Barry sizes him up.

BARRY: You know, I had pegged you for a golfer!

[… double-entendre exchange omitted from excerpt …]

Barry lets all of the air out of his lungs without breaking his smile.

[…]

The old guest spots a pamphlet for a local yoga class. […] He storms off.

Barry looks uncomfortable. He grabs all of the yoga pamphlets as if they're covered in vomit and throws them away.

END TEASER

ACT ONE

INT. BARRY'S OFFICE - DAY

Cramped. Filing cabinets, a desk, five years worth of employee of the month plaques line one wall. Barry's knees come up past his waist just for him to fit behind the desk.

EVAN (29), part-time bell hop, full-time ladies man, sits back, feet up, and uses one of Barry's employee of the month plaques to as a mirror to clean his teeth. Evan looks around.

EVAN: Jeeze Barry, why is your office so small?

BARRY: Remember I had it zoned off to make another guest room back here.

Barry thumbs at the wall behind him. Evan looks at the wall to other room and grins.

EVAN: Oh! I remember.

[… flashback gag omitted …]

BARRY: I still can't work nights without thinking about what you did.

Evan nods, pleased with himself. The phone rings.

BARRY (CONT'D): Evan, can you pick that up?

Evan looks around confused, picks up the entire rotary phone box and searches under it. Barry reaches over and takes the receiver and puts it up to Evan's ear.

EVAN: Yeah? Uh huh. Alright cool.

Evan hangs up the phone.

BARRY: What was that about?

EVAN: I don't really know. They said something about Mr. McAllister and a helicopter.

BARRY: Mr. McAllister!? The owner?!

EVAN: (straining) Maybe?

[…]

INT. PENTHOUSE SUITE - DAY

Barry sits on the edge of the bed, a bag of ice on his head. Evan huddles over him and McAllister stands at a distance.

[…]

BARRY: No I'm fine, Mister McAl-

MCALLISTER: Don't you dare call me Melvin.

BARRY: (confused) Umm okay. Like I was saying you're Mister McAllister.

MCALLISTER: My father called me Melvin.

He stares at Barry intensely.

BARRY: Right. Um. As I was trying to say it is such an honor to have our hotel's owner in house. (giddy) And to finally meet you! What is the occasion?

MCALLISTER: Your entire staff is fired.

BARRY: Did you say fired?

MCALLISTER: Well not fired per se. […]

BARRY: (close to tears) I don't want to go to Nigeria.

MCALLISTER: Don't be silly. I need at least one man left who knows luxury.

BARRY: (complete 180) Well that's a relief!

[…]

INT. STAFF CONFERENCE ROOM - DAY

Barry looks around at the new employees with a look like he just smelled a dirty diaper.

[…]

BARRY: (forced enthusiasm) Hello! My name is Barry Howard. I am the Hotel Manager. I have been here at the Palace for the past eleven years and have been manager for the past five, sweeping the employee of the month competition ever since. I enjoy caring for my pet Beta fish Logan, studying aviation manuscripts and I even dabble in replicating my favorite recipes from the Food Network.

[…]

BILL: Hi there. My name is Bill Cullen, and I'm an alcoholic.

ALL: (droning) Hi Bill.

BARRY: It's not that kind of meeting guys.

BILL: I am the head of...

Bill pulls out a notecard.

BILL (CONT'D): (sounding out) Main...Ten...Ants.

BARRY: Maintenance.

MCALLISTER: Bill, you know a thing or two about construction?

BILL: Do I? I was a contractor for eight years. Would have been longer if it weren't for that Dairy Queen collapsing on that boy. It only took him two years to walk again. Hell, I even built my own patio out of rebar, wood chippings, and Playdoh.

MCALLISTER: That sounds good enough for me. You interested in helping out on renovations for my office?

BILL: Gee would I.

MCALLISTER: A real go-getter! I like that. You know what, you can call me Melvin.

Barry fumes.
